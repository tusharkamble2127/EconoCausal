# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Infotact Solutions Internship Collaboration Guidelines. All team members are expected to maintain a professional, respectful, and collaborative environment throughout the development of the EconoCausal AI project.

* **Mutual Respect:** Treat all team members with professionalism, respect, and courtesy.
* **Professional Behavior:** Maintain responsible and ethical behavior during project discussions, development, and presentations.
* **Active Engagement:** Participate actively in meetings, discussions, reviews, and project activities.
* **Responsibility:** Complete assigned tasks on time and take ownership of the quality of your work.
* **Open Communication:** Communicate progress, challenges, and concerns clearly with the team.
* **Constructive Collaboration:** Accept feedback positively and resolve disagreements through respectful discussion.
* **Data and Project Responsibility:** Handle project data, code, documentation, and other project resources responsibly and maintain appropriate confidentiality.
* **Conflict Resolution:** Team members should first attempt to resolve conflicts constructively within the team. A mentor should be involved only when a resolution cannot be reached.
* **Zero Tolerance for Harassment:** Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or disparagement based on race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, or any other personal characteristic will not be tolerated and will be reported according to the Infotact Solutions SOP reporting process.
